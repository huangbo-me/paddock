# PadDock Afdian Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans inline with one final independent review.

**Goal:** Deliver a 0.2.0 beta with Afdian purchase entry, offline license activation, 14-day trial and an issuer tool.
**Architecture:** Pure Swift/CryptoKit licensing; Keychain persistence; SwiftUI billing page; separate command-line issuer. No payment server or merchant credentials.
**Tech Stack:** Swift, CryptoKit, Security, existing native app.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-22-afdian.md

## Constraints
- No live shop exists; purchasing must be visibly unavailable until configured.
- Never disconnect an active screen on entitlement changes; paired headless recovery stays available.
- No private signing key in distributed artifacts; licenses are transferable offline, not device limited.
- Installed 0.1.0 and its settings remain untouched.

## Review Focus
- Forged/malformed/wrong-version licenses rejected without replacing valid activation.
- Trial survives preference resets; Keychain failures do not reset it.
- Headless-only users are not stranded by trial expiration or keychain errors.
- Blank or attacker-controlled purchase URL never launches.
- Issuer never overwrites key/inventory; end-to-end codes validate against shipped public key.
